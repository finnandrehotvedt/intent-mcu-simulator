# Community conduct

Intent MCU Simulator welcomes constructive participation from people of all
backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Make room for learners and explain assumptions when they affect safety.
- Respect privacy, copyright, licences, and project security boundaries.
- Clearly label uncertainty and avoid presenting simulated behavior as proof
  about physical hardware.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
credential disclosure, deliberate safety misrepresentation, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or restrict
participation when needed to protect the community and project. For a concern
that is safe to discuss publicly, open a repository issue. For a private safety
or security concern, use the repository's private **Report a vulnerability**
route. Reports are reviewed on their substance, regardless of the reporter's
role or familiarity with the project.
