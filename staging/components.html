Model limits matter

Sensors use ideal bounded values, contacts do not model bounce or wear, light and sound outputs are relative indicators, and the LCD implements a tested command subset. The thermostat experiment uses the temperature module only as an ideal linear signal, its button as an ideal active-low contact and its LED as a heater-command indicator; it does not model a room or physical heater. Representable invalid polarity, missing current limiting, ground, known output contention, named-rail shorts and unsupported topologies fail closed with corrective actions. These checks are not physical-damage prediction or safety certification, and arbitrary cross-controller voltage wiring is unavailable.

Controller compatibility matrix

There are 19 unique public models and 76 profile/model cells. AVR has 16 available models: 13 Simulated and 3 Partial. Each narrow controller profile has exactly 3 available models: its Partial controller plus the Simulated resistor and LED. Across the complete matrix, 25 cells are available and 51 are unavailable.

AVR project modes: ordinary project-v2 shows 5 Simulated parts. Candidate-v3 shows 13 by default; choose All statuses to see all 16, including 3 Partial models. Partial always means the stated bounded behaviour, never broad hardware equivalence.

Control persistence: validated AVR button, potentiometer, switch, sensor, threshold and encoder values are canonical project data. Open never restores runtime events or executable authority and always requires a fresh Build. The current narrow profiles have no interactive control keys.

Pico, ESP32-C3 and STM32 supported parts

The general AVR editor supports click-add, drag placement and disconnected duplication with fresh stable IDs. Each narrow workbench filters the panel to exactly three entries: its fixed controller, resistor.fixed-v1 and led.basic-v1. Narrow controllers cannot be added, duplicated or deleted. A missing resistor or LED is restored with the same stable ID and deterministic safe placement, but with no inferred cable.

Fixed controller

Pico exposes GP0 physical pin 1 and GND pin 3; ESP32-C3 exposes GPIO2 J1 pin 4 and GND J1 pin 6; STM32F429ZI exposes PB0 CN11 pin 34 and GND CN11 pin 35. These are profile-specific, not interchangeable.

Series resistor

The resistance field is accepted only when the selected LED remains inside the exact ideal 3.3 V / 12 mA model. Unsafe values reject atomically and leave the previous project unchanged.

LED colour

Red, yellow, green and blue select tested ideal forward-voltage constants. Colour is not a wavelength, brightness, tolerance, thermal or physical-accuracy claim.

Recovery rule

After adding a missing part, activate compatible free named pins to recreate each cable. The workbench never guesses a connection. Every accepted part or wire edit retires stale firmware until a fresh Build.
