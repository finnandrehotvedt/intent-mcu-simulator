Tested AVR subset

  • Digital GPIO D0–D13, input, output, and pull-up modes
  • ADC A0–A5 against bounded 0–5 V sources
  • PWM on D3, D5, D6, D9, D10, and D11
  • USART0, bounded timing, deterministic reset, and instruction stepping
  • INT0/INT1 and the implemented pin-change masks

Not claimed

Complete cycle-perfect hardware equivalence, physical-device upload, every library, SPI, I²C/TWI, EEPROM writes, watchdog activation, analogue-comparator use, and electrical-safety certification are not part of this release.

Why the three previews stay private

The bounded private proofs are not production multi-user services. Public execution remains disabled until fair admission, per-origin/session rate and concurrency limits, abuse accounting, time/body/output limits, memory-only transient projects, cancellation cleanup, networkless non-privileged isolation, and an availability/operations contract pass release acceptance. The C3 compiler worker must also run non-root.
