Blink LED

Connect D13 through a 220 Ω resistor to an LED and ground. Build, run, and compare the light with GPIO and Serial ON/OFF records.

Button input

Read a tactile button on D2 with INPUT_PULLUP. Press it in Interact mode and inspect firmware-derived input and Serial state.

ADC dial

Turn a potentiometer feeding A0 and compare its ideal 0–5 V position with the 10-bit ADC values produced by executed firmware.

PWM fade

Drive a current-limited LED from D3 and relate duty cycle, relative brightness, and the logic analyser waveform.

Temperature display

Read the ideal temperature module on A1, write whole Celsius to a 16×2 LCD, and select a current-limited RGB status colour.
